NFC PCB Business Card

PCB Design | KiCad | NFC Technology | Soldering

I designed and assembled a custom PCB business card that uses Near Field Communication (NFC) technology to connect physical hardware with my digital portfolio. By tapping the card with a compatible smartphone, users can access my website, LinkedIn, resume, and engineering projects.

Design & Development Process

01. Schematic Design

I began by designing the circuit schematic in KiCad, selecting and connecting the components needed for the NFC functionality.

02. PCB Layout

After completing the schematic, I transferred the design into KiCad's PCB Editor. I arranged the components and routed the electrical connections to create the physical board layout.

03. PCB Manufacturing

Once the design was complete, I prepared the manufacturing files and ordered the custom PCB for fabrication.

04. Assembly & Soldering

After receiving the manufactured PCB, I soldered the electronic components onto the board to assemble the physical business card.

Project Outcome

This project gave me hands-on experience with the PCB development process, from schematic capture and board layout to fabrication and component assembly. It also allowed me to combine my interest in hardware design with a practical networking application.
